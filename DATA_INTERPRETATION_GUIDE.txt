FLEETSIGHT — DATA CONSTRAINTS, RANGES, AND RESULT INTERPRETATION GUIDE
For reviewer / presentation use
Dataset loaded: datasets.zip (validated and imported locally)

1. DATA COVERAGE AND IMPORTANT CONTEXT
The imported package contains 300 machines, 5,000 rental transactions, 100,000
telemetry events, 1,500 maintenance events, and 120 customers.

Historical coverage:
- Rentals: check-ins from 01 Jan 2025 to 30 Jun 2025; final actual returns up to 07 Aug 2025.
- Telemetry: 01 Jan 2025 00:01 to 01 Jan 2026 00:00.
- Maintenance: 01 Jul 2024 to 01 Jul 2025.
- Machine master service dates: 31 May 2026 to 30 Jul 2026.

Constraint: this is a historical/synthetic dataset. The dashboard’s current-date
alerts should be described as an operational demonstration, not a live claim.
For example, a past rental labelled Overdue remains an important historical risk;
it is not evidence that a machine is overdue today unless the rental is active.

2. SOURCE DATA RANGES AND WHAT THEY MEAN
MACHINE MASTER
- Manufacture year: 2016–2025. Older equipment should receive closer service
  review when its telemetry/condition also worsens.
- Machine age: 1.0–10.9 years; median 6.1 years.
- Fuel tank capacity: 73–649 L; do not compare fuel litres between machines
  without considering tank size. The dashboard normally uses fuel percentage.
- Maximum operating hours per day: 8–12 hours; a daily engine-hours value over
  the machine-specific maximum is a potential heavy-usage/misuse signal.
- Machine health score: 51–98 (median 75). Higher is healthier.
- Service interval: 30–60 days. A service date plus this interval gives the
  expected next-service date.
- Fleet status: 134 Rented, 119 Available, 30 Idle, 17 Maintenance.

RENTALS
- Rental duration: 2–30 days; median 16 days.
- Daily rate: Rs 12,000–Rs 30,000; median Rs 22,000.
- Total cost: Rs 24,000–Rs 1,140,000; median Rs 336,000.
- Deposit: Rs 24,000–Rs 60,000.
- Recorded overdue days: 0–10 days.
- Contract labels: 4,014 Completed and 986 Overdue.
Interpretation: an overdue count measures return/contract risk. Pair it with
customer late-return count and risk level before deciding on escalation.

TELEMETRY
- Daily engine hours: 0.00–12.00; median 5.99 hours.
- Total engine hours: 500–15,000; median 7,714. High accumulated hours indicate
  greater wear and should be considered with maintenance history.
- Daily idle hours: 0.00–11.95; median 2.23 hours. High idle means fuel waste
  and lower utilization, but is not proof of misuse without site context.
- Fuel level: 5.0–100%; median 52.8%. Low fuel creates dispatch/refuelling risk.
- Fuel consumed per reading: 0.00–239.78 L; median 78.51 L. Compare this with
  the telemetry period and tank capacity; it is not automatically a fuel-theft
  measurement.
- Engine temperature: 70.0–110.0°C; median 90.0°C. Persistently high readings
  can indicate cooling-system or loading problems.
- Hydraulic pressure: 180.0–320.0 bar; median 250.3 bar. Interpret against the
  manufacturer’s model-specific safe pressure, which is not supplied here.
- Vibration score: 0.50–8.00; median 4.24. A rising/high score can imply wear,
  imbalance, poor ground conditions, or operator/attachment effects.
- RPM: 700–2,200; median 1,452. RPM requires engine/load context, so it is
  displayed as a trend rather than a standalone alert threshold.
- Distance from site: 0.0–5.0 km. Geofence labels: 94,945 Inside and 5,055
  Outside. Outside is an immediate location/security review signal.
- Movement labels: 55,033 Working, 19,919 Idle, 15,068 Travelling, 9,980 Stopped.
- Dataset-provided alert labels: High Temperature 24,865; High Vibration 17,958;
  Low Fuel 7,727; Geofence Breach 2,530; Maintenance Due 934.
- Telemetry condition score: 54–100; median 80. This is the source score. The
  app also calculates a transparent demo health score for latest readings.

MAINTENANCE AND CUSTOMERS
- Maintenance downtime: 2.0–48.0 hours; median 25.5 hours.
- Service cost: Rs 5,055–Rs 149,875; median Rs 82,092.
- Customer total rentals: 1–49; customer spend: Rs 97,254–Rs 6,581,542.
- Late-return count: 0–8. A high late-return count combined with High risk level
  supports deposit/approval review, not automatic customer rejection.

3. APPLICATION MONITORING CONSTRAINTS AND EXPECTED RESULT
The following are configurable environment thresholds in .env. They are initial
demo controls and should be calibrated per equipment model and site.

- TEMPERATURE > 105°C -> Critical “High temperature” alert.
  Result: investigate cooling, oil, loading, sensor quality, and stop the asset
  if manufacturer policy requires it.
- IDLE HOURS > 5 hours/day -> Warning “Excessive idle” alert.
  Result: review utilization, operator behavior, queue time, or site access.
- FUEL CONSUMED > 20 L per telemetry observation -> Warning “Fuel loss” alert.
  Result: compare with prior reading, engine hours, tank capacity, and refuelling
  logs. This is an unusual-consumption screen, not a theft verdict.
- GEOFENCE is Outside -> Critical “Geofence exit” alert.
  Result: verify transport authorization, site boundary, GPS accuracy, and asset
  security immediately.
- Expected return is today or earlier for an ACTIVE rental -> Critical overdue
  alert; within two days -> warning due-soon alert.
  Result: contact customer/operator and plan collection or extension.
- Last service + service interval within 7 days -> Maintenance-due warning.
  Result: schedule service around the rental plan to avoid unplanned downtime.

Alert controls de-duplicate an unresolved alert with the same machine/category
for 12 hours. This prevents repeated telemetry from flooding the operations team.

4. HEALTH SCORE BANDS USED IN THE APP
The application’s transparent demo health score starts at 100 and applies
penalties for high temperature, idle time, vibration above 5, a fault code, and
geofence exit. It is intentionally explainable rather than a black-box model.

- 80–100: Healthy — normal monitoring.
- 65–79: Watch — inspect trend and schedule a review.
- 45–64: Warning — prioritize inspection and corrective action.
- 0–44: Critical — operational intervention is recommended.

The score is a decision-support indicator. It must not replace manufacturer
service specifications, certified inspection, or safety policy.

5. MACHINE-LEARNING ANOMALY VIEW
Isolation Forest is trained on engine hours, idle hours, fuel consumed,
temperature, and vibration. It marks approximately 12% of telemetry readings as
statistical outliers. An outlier is unusual relative to this dataset; it is not
automatically a fault. Review it together with the rule alerts, fault code,
movement status, site conditions, and trend history.

7. LIMITATIONS TO STATE OPENLY
- Dataset labels and dates are synthetic/historical, so no live GPS or IoT claim
  is made.
- Thresholds need manufacturer/site calibration before production use.
- The 18% forecast buffer is a transparent baseline, not a trained forecast.
- The supplied rental feed has no customer_name column; the importer safely joins
  it from Customer_Master.csv using customer_id.
- Future production work: sensor validation, FCM/email delivery adapter,
  scheduled workers, RBAC authorization per action, audit logs, and migrations.

9. INTEGRITY LEDGER NOTE — 30 JULY 2026
Historical data anchors are calculated from the exact imported records. A verified
anchor means the current snapshot matches its latest approved hash proof; it does
not mean the operational data is automatically correct. A mismatch means records
changed after anchoring and must be reviewed against approved imports/corrections.
The proof ledger contains hashes only, never the values described in this guide.

10. LIVE MAP NOTE — 30 JULY 2026
Latitude/longitude values are rendered as latest equipment markers and short recent
movement lines in the Leaflet map. A line represents the chronological sampled GPS
history from the dataset; it is not a road-verified route. GPS position must be
shown only within the authorised tenant/customer scope in production.
