FLEETSIGHT - PROJECT SUMMARY
Status as of 06:00 PM, 30 July 2026

* Tools & Technology Used
  - Python and Streamlit for the interactive, responsive FleetSight dashboard.
  - Pandas, Plotly and SQLAlchemy for data processing, visual analytics and a portable database layer (SQLite locally, PostgreSQL-ready for deployment).
  - Scikit-learn Isolation Forest and XGBoost for anomaly detection and equipment-risk prediction; TensorFlow/Keras LSTM models for demand and telemetry trend forecasting.
  - Leaflet with OpenStreetMap for interactive equipment location tracking and movement history.
  - SHA-256 Merkle proofs and a tenant-aware hash chain for historical-data integrity, with an optional Hyperledger Fabric integration path.
  - Passlib/bcrypt for secure password hashing, with role-based demo accounts.

* How We Plan to Use AI
  - AI turns raw rental, maintenance and IoT-style telemetry data into early operational signals instead of waiting for a machine to fail or a rental issue to escalate.
  - The system detects unusual behaviour, estimates maintenance, overdue-return and misuse risk, classifies machine health, and forecasts upcoming demand by site and equipment type.
  - Results are designed as decision support: managers can investigate high-risk assets, plan servicing, improve fleet allocation and reduce avoidable downtime. The models will be continuously retrained and calibrated when verified live operational data becomes available.

* Our Approach to the Problem
  - We approached fleet operations as one connected workflow: asset availability, rental lifecycle, location, equipment condition, maintenance and customer return risk are viewed from a single command centre.
  - We combined clear business rules (temperature, idle time, fuel use, geofence and service thresholds) with machine-learning signals, so the system remains both practical and explainable.
  - The solution is privacy-conscious: sensitive raw GPS, financial and customer information stays in the operational database, while only cryptographic proofs are used for integrity verification.
  - The current imported dataset is historical/synthetic, so the product is positioned honestly as a validated operational prototype that is ready to connect to live data.

* Key Features & Unique Selling Point
  - One unified dashboard for heavy-equipment rentals: live fleet KPIs, asset condition, rentals, alerts, maintenance, analytics, forecasting and downloadable reports.
  - Interactive live map with colour-coded asset markers, health context and compact recent movement paths.
  - Automated monitoring agents for excessive idling, high temperature, geofence exits, unusual fuel consumption, overdue rentals and maintenance due dates.
  - Hybrid intelligence: transparent health scoring plus anomaly detection, XGBoost risk models and LSTM forecasts.
  - Multi-role secure login for admin, manager, operator and customer demonstrations.
  - Unique selling point: FleetSight brings predictive fleet intelligence and privacy-preserving, tamper-evident historical data verification together in one rental-operations platform.
